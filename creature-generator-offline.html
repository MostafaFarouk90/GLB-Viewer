Creature Generator

Procedural animals and monsters, exported as GLB, static or rigged with Idle, Walk and other clips. Companion of the props and character generators. Open the result in the GLB viewer & optimizer to check or shrink it.

Game

Embers of Valdren animals and foes. Picking one sets the species, the colours, the target size, the triangle budget and game-ready export settings. None of these ids is in targets.json yet, so their sizes are estimates until the game adds them.

Creature

The same species and seed always give the same creature.

Output

None: one static mesh, as the game wants scenery. Jointed parts: one node per joint (Hips, Neck, Head, LegFL1…) holding its own piece, for a procedural rig like the game's quadruped. Skeleton: one skinned mesh on bones with the same names, each part bound rigidly to its bone.
With a rig: the clips listed below, looping, named as the game's quadruped clips (Idle, Walk, Run, Bite).
Flat colours as the game wants them: roughness 1, metallic 0. Glow parts keep their emissive colour.
