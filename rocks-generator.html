Rock and Cliff Kit

Procedural cliffs, ledges, scree, caves, arches, crags, boulders and golem rock chunks, exported as GLB. Smaller props are in the props generator; open any result in the GLB viewer & optimizer to check or change it.

Game

Embers of Valdren rock ids. Picking one sets the piece, the game palette, the target size (targets.json, or a provisional size for ids the game has not named yet), the triangle budget and game-ready export settings.

Piece

The same type, size and seed always give the same piece.

Shape

WHD
Width along X, height, depth along Z. The piece is built at exactly this size.
