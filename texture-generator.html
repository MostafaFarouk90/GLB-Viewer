Texture & Decal Generator

Procedural 512 px game-palette textures that tile, alpha decals for the ground, and a palette atlas that turns a many-material model into one texture. For the props and character generators and the GLB viewer & optimizer.

Game

Embers of Valdren decals from the missing-objects list. Picking one sets the decal, the game palette and its size, and the download is a flat <id>.glb ready for the game.

Texture

The same type and seed always give the same image. Textures tile seamlessly; decals fade to transparent at the edge.

Output

The GLB is a flat square on the ground at y 0, centred on x 0, z 0, with the top of the image towards −Z. Roughness 1, metallic 0, base colour only.
The pack has every texture and decal with this seed and palette at the chosen size, as PNG, plus manifest.json with the settings to regenerate each one.

Palette atlas (from a model)

Open a GLB with several materials. Every flat colour becomes a swatch and every texture whose UVs stay inside 0 to 1 is copied into one atlas image; the UVs are moved onto it, so the model needs one material (plus one per glow colour). Roughness 1, metallic 0.
Off: textured materials become their average colour, so the atlas is swatches only.
