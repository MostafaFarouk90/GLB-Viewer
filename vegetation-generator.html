Vegetation Generator

Procedural trees, bushes, hedges, grass, ferns, flowers and reeds in a chunky low-poly style, exported as GLB with LODs. Rocks, stumps and logs are in the props generator; open any result in the GLB viewer & optimizer to check or change it.

Game

Embers of Valdren vegetation from the missing-objects list (sections A and F). Picking one sets the plant type, the game palette, the exact target size from targets.json, the triangle budget and game-ready export settings.

LODs

Simplified copies for distant instances: name.glb, name.lod1.glb, name.lod2.glb. A 6,000-face oak gives about 1,500 and 300: the same plant rebuilt coarser with fewer blossoms and marks, then simplified. Leaf clusters keep their volume, so a small plant's LOD can stay above its percentage. Preview them with "Show" above the view.
