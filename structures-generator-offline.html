Structures Kit Generator

Modular structure pieces (walls, roofs, chimneys, porches, scaffolds, gates), mine and forge works, shrines, wells and wagons, exported as GLB. Whole buildings come from the KayKit packs; this kit fills the gaps. See also the props generator and the GLB viewer & optimizer.

Game

Embers of Valdren objects from the missing-objects list. Picking one sets the type, the game palette, the exact target size from targets.json, the triangle budget and game-ready export settings.

Piece

The same type and seed always give the same piece.

Shape

As designed, pieces are built to real sizes (a door is 2.1 m high), so walls, corners and roofs of the same length and height fit together.
